Grading instructions for the teaching sheet (final matching measurement).

Judge only from the text shown: the skill name and the item text on the row.
Do not look anything up, and do not use what you know about the item from
elsewhere. You are not told which version of the app produced a row.

### judgements/teaching.csv

Columns:

```
row_id,career_goal_id,gap,item_type,item_id,item_title,item_text,grade,note
```

- `item_type` is opportunity (a course, programme, bootcamp or event) or
  certification.
- **[S7]** Some rows come from career goals that have no CV in the set. Grade
  them the same way: the grade depends only on the skill name and the item
  text, never on a CV.
- `grade`:
  - 2: the item teaches the skill as one of its main topics;
  - 1: the item teaches it as a clear part;
  - 0: it does not teach it (a passing mention, a different meaning of the
    same word, or only a prerequisite).

  Example (telecom, not in any set): for "RF Planning", a 5G radio network
  design course is 2, a general telecom fundamentals course with one unit on
  radio planning is 1, and a fibre splicing course is 0.

  **[S7]** When the skill name itself packs several things, grade 1 or 2 only
  if the item teaches the main part of what the name says, and write
  "bundled name" in `note`.
