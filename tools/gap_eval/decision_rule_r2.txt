## 7. Decision rule for the final measurement (fixed now, copied word for word into tools/gap_eval/decision_rule_r2.txt and hashed)

All numbers on the decision names of 5.2, strata M and W together
**[V12]** (the draft's "all of them are in unseen goals" no longer holds:
W names can belong to goals seen in development; what makes them new is
the name-level exclusion list). "Today" means the frozen A0. TeachHit@5, AttribPrec@5 and FalseUncovered
are defined as in hardest/plan_final.md section 4.3: teachable means some
pooled item is graded at least 1 in that catalogue, and each name's value is
the mean over its link runs.

7.1 Feasibility gate (before measuring): no new service or key; offline at
recommendation time; app size +2 MB at most; no new native dependency; for
C also at most +10 s analysis time at the 90th percentile in the development
smoke run.

7.2 Hard guards, each must pass:
(a) An analysis without gapLinks gives exactly today's opportunity and
    certification lists: unit tests, and evaluate_recommender without links
    equal to today at 3 decimals on both persona files.
(b) Persona guard with links (evaluate_recommender, the frozen link prompt,
    mean over 3 link runs), both persona files: nDCG@10, GapCov@10 and P@5
    each at least today's value minus 0.02, and TopGapRank at most today's
    value plus 0.02. **[V1]** This tolerance is new in round 2; round 1's
    rule was "not lower than today" (TopGapRank not higher). It is frozen
    only if the orchestrator shows, before the freeze, that the user's own
    instruction for this round set it, and the freeze file records that
    source. Otherwise the frozen text is round 1's rule with tolerance 0.
    Today: development 0.837 / 0.933 / 1.000 / 1.25,
    held-out 0.806 / 0.750 / 0.975 / 1.50. An item that newly enters the
    first 10 without a label counts as not relevant. If the guard fails only
    because of such items, the result is "pending": the candidate does not
    ship unless the user approves blind labelling of those items (labels
    added, never removed) and the guard then passes.
(c) AttribPrec@5 >= 0.70 for opportunities and >= 0.70 for certifications,
    on the decision names (micro over names, each name's link runs weigh 1
    in total, as round 1's scorer). A catalogue with no attribution at all
    passes. The guard is the point estimate, as in round 1; its 90%
    interval and the number of attributions behind it are printed next to
    it (reported, not a decision input).
(d) Timing (TIMING block of evaluate_recommender, same process, 21
    repetitions alternating today and the candidate, medians): recommend()
    with the index built at most 2 x today's median; the first call on a new
    list at most 1.10 x today's.

7.3 Adoption: TeachHit@5 on the decision names (mean of the two catalogues)
beats A0 by at least 0.10, with the 95% bootstrap interval of the difference
above 0 (10,000 resamples of names within (stratum, goal) **[V12]**, a name
with both catalogues as one unit, seed 20261121); and in neither catalogue
is the candidate below A0 (point estimate).

7.4 Winner: the highest TeachHit@5 among frozen candidates that pass 7.2 and
7.3. If two are within noise (the interval of their difference includes 0):
lower FalseUncovered, then fewer requests per analysis (A = B < C), then
fewer moving parts. An X-opp fallback can win only if its full candidate
fails.

7.5 No winner: A0 stays and the link code is switched off (8.6).

7.6 Control: if the winner's TeachHit@5 is not above L1g's (point estimate),
the report says that the gain is not shown to come from meaning, and the
user decides before anything ships.

7.7 Reported, never used to decide: **[V12]** every metric per stratum (M
and W) and the size report; the names that were a G1E1 gap of a final-set
CV; the reason lines of whole analyses of the final-set CVs (all gaps at
once, as the app shows them); AttribPrec and TeachHit per goal; L1, and M1
where available; FalseUncovered; the share of the gain over A0 kept relative
to M1 where M1 is available; **[V14]** for B and C, a note that the link (or
verification) answers and the grades both come from AI models with similar
notions of "teaches", so agreement between them may look better than a
human reader would find it.
